Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Cram Arcade is a free study app made by Shashank J Reddy. It's built to collect as little about you as possible. There are no ads, we don't sell or rent anyone's information, and we don't use tracking cookies.

Stored only on your device

By default, everything stays in your browser on your own device: your study sets, tickets, power-ups, best times, tests, study sessions, and nickname. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Stored on our servers, only if you use these features

  • Crews: your nickname, nameplate, tickets earned in that crew, and boss wins, so your crew's leaderboard works. Crews are deleted after 180 days with no activity.
  • Classes: the first name you enter, when you joined, and your results on the sets your teacher assigned (scores, boss battles, flashcard progress, and which questions you got wrong). Your teacher can see these. Classes are deleted after a year with no activity, and teachers can remove students at any time.
  • Signing in with Microsoft or Google: your name and email address (and Google profile picture link, if you use Google), plus a copy of your progress so it syncs between devices. We never see or store your Microsoft or Google password.

This information is stored with Vercel (which hosts the app) and Upstash (our database), in the United States.

Make it with AI

When you use "Make it with AI," the notes or topic you enter are sent to Anthropic's Claude to create your study set. We don't keep your notes on our servers. Anthropic handles them under its privacy policy. Please don't paste in passwords or private details about yourself or other people.

Your calendar

If you choose "Find tests in Outlook" or "Find tests in Google Calendar," the app reads your upcoming calendar events in your browser, read-only, to look for tests. Only the tests you choose to track are saved (their name, date, and any short notes on the event). We never change your calendar.

Technical information

Like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address when you load the app. We use it briefly to stop people from making too many AI study sets too quickly, and we don't use it to identify you.

Students

Cram Arcade is meant for students 13 and older. Teachers using it with a class should follow their school's rules for student apps. We only ask students for a first name or nickname, never a last name or email (unless they choose to sign in).

Deleting your information

  • Leave a crew or class from inside the app, or ask your teacher to remove you from a class.
  • To have your account or any other information deleted, contact us below and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top and note it in the app.
